Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive look at the sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution of a leading quick-commerce platform. The report is designed to help stakeholders identify growth opportunities and optimize operational efficiency through data-driven insights.



&#x20;

Key Features \& Insights

Total Revenue \& Sales Performance: Tracks top-line growth with high-level metrics on total sales and average transaction values.



Customer Satisfaction Analysis: Visualizes average ratings and feedback trends to monitor service quality.



Inventory \& Product Distribution: Analyzes the performance of various item types—from perishables to household essentials—to understand the product mix.



Outlet Optimization: Compares sales across different outlet sizes (Small, Medium, High) and locations (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3) to identify the most profitable geographical segments.



Trend Identification: Uses time-series analysis to spotlight peak ordering periods and seasonal fluctuations in the grocery market.
