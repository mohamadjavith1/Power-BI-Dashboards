\# \*\*📡 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard\*\*



\## 📊 Project Overview



This Power BI dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of customer churn in a telecom business. It is designed to help identify churn patterns, understand customer behavior, and support retention strategies using data-driven insights.



The report is structured into multiple analytical views, allowing users to explore churn from different perspectives such as customer profile, services used, and usage patterns.



\---



\## 📌 Dashboard Structure



\### 🧭 1. Overview



Provides a high-level summary of customer data:



\* Total Customers: \*\*7,043\*\*

\* Customers Churned: \*\*2,000\*\*

\* Average Monthly Charges: \*\*64.76\*\*

\* Average Tenure: \*\*32.37\*\*



\*\*Key Insights:\*\*



\* Majority of customers use Fiber Optic services

\* Monthly contracts dominate customer base

\* Electronic check is the most preferred payment method

\* Gender distribution is almost equal



\---



\### 📉 2. Churn Analysis



Focuses on identifying churn patterns across key categories:



\* \*\*Churn by Contract Type\*\*



&#x20; \* Higher churn observed in \*\*Monthly contracts\*\*



\* \*\*Churn by Internet Service\*\*



&#x20; \* Fiber optic users show higher churn compared to DSL



\* \*\*Churn by Payment Method\*\*



&#x20; \* Electronic check users have higher churn tendency



\* \*\*Churn by Gender\*\*



&#x20; \* Churn distribution is similar across male and female customers



\---



\### 🔧 3. Churn by Services



Analyzes how additional services impact churn:



\* Phone Service

\* Online Security

\* Tech Support

\* Streaming TV

\* Movie Streaming

\* Device Protection



\*\*Key Insights:\*\*



\* Customers without \*\*online security\*\* and \*\*tech support\*\* show higher churn

\* Lack of additional services increases churn risk

\* Value-added services improve customer retention



\---



\### 📊 4. Usage Analysis



Provides detailed customer-level data including:



\* Customer ID

\* Gender

\* Internet Service

\* Payment Method

\* Monthly Charges

\* Tenure

\* Total Charges



Includes filters for:



\* Partner

\* Internet Service

\* Payment Method



\*\*Purpose:\*\*



\* Enables granular exploration of customer behavior

\* Helps identify high-risk customers
