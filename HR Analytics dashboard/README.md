# 👥 Workforce Attrition & Employee Satisfaction Analysis

**Access Live Dashboard Here:** https://tinyurl.com/2hvzjpp5

## 📊 Project Overview
This end-to-end Power BI project analyzes a large-scale workforce of nearly 60,000 employees to uncover the underlying drivers behind attrition and employee satisfaction. The goal of this solution is to transform raw, siloed HR data into actionable insights, enabling leadership to optimize retention strategies and enhance overall organizational health.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Executive Overview
* **Purpose:** Provides high-level KPIs for executive leadership.
* **Metrics:** Total Headcount, overall Attrition Rate, Average Salary, and Average Tenure.
* **Strategic Insight:** Delivers an immediate, single-pane snapshot of the company’s organizational scale and workforce stability.

### 2. Attrition & Retention Analysis
* **Purpose:** Identifies the "why" and "where" behind employee departures.
* **Metrics:** Attrition broken down by Job Role, Overtime impact, and Distance from Home.
* **Strategic Insight:** Highlights high-risk departments and isolates the direct correlation between work-life balance factors and employee turnover.

### 3. Compensation & Performance Evaluation
* **Purpose:** Analyzes pay equity, promotion fairness, and career growth.
* **Metrics:** Monthly Income by Gender and Job Level, paired with the link between Performance Ratings and Promotion frequency.
* **Strategic Insight:** Benchmarks corporate transparency in pay scales and objectively evaluates if the organization is effectively "promoting from within."

### 4. Demographics & Work Environment
* **Purpose:** Maps out workforce diversity, remote adoption, and corporate culture.
* **Metrics:** Remote work adoption rates, age distribution, and employee sentiment analysis (*Job Satisfaction & Company Reputation*).
* **Strategic Insight:** Assists HR leaders in tailoring culture-building and wellness initiatives based on actual employee feedback and changing demographic needs.

## ⚙️ Technical Toolkit & Architecture

* **Advanced DAX Measures:** Developed context-aware metrics for **Attrition %** using robust `CALCULATE` and safety-checked `DIVIDE` functions to handle dynamic cross-filtering flawlessly.
* **Data Transformation (ETL):** Cleaned, normalized, and structured a dense dataset of 24+ columns in Power Query to minimize file size and maximize calculation speed.
* **UI/UX & Visualization:** Engineered a cohesive design mixing heatmaps, distribution charts, and high-impact KPI cards to guarantee instant data scannability for business stakeholders.
