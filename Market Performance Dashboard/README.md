# 💼 Executive Financial Performance Insights Dashboard

**Access Live Dashboard Here:** https://tinyurl.com/5n7dmynb

## 📊 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a strategic, comprehensive view of organizational financial health, engineered to help executives and finance managers track critical performance indicators in real time. By integrating internal financial metrics with macroeconomic indicators, the report bridges the gap between high-level executive summaries and granular trend analyses to drive data-backed decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Visuals

* **Core Financial Metrics:** High-impact KPI cards displaying top-line and bottom-line health, including **Total Sales**, **Gross Profit**, and **Net Income**.
* **Economic Context Tracking:** Integrates external economic indicators—such as inflation rates, interest rates, and GDP growth—to benchmark internal performance against broader market conditions.
* **Market & Commodity Analysis:** Tracks currency fluctuations (e.g., USD/EUR, USD/JPY) and critical commodity prices (e.g., Gold, Crude Oil) to monitor the direct impact of market volatility on margins.
* **Trend & Investment Analysis:** Employs advanced time-series visualizations focusing on sales growth trajectories, corporate spending patterns, and venture capital investment trends.
* **Operational Health Metrics:** Monitors labor market impacts via unemployment rate tracking juxtaposed against corporate profit margins.

---

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights

* **Data Modeling:** Leverages a robust **Star Schema** architecture to handle multi-fact granular data, ensuring high-speed cross-filtering and scalability.
* **Advanced DAX Measures:** Utilizes custom DAX formulas for dynamic Year-over-Year (YoY) growth calculations, rolling averages, and context-aware profit margin analyses.
* **UI/UX Design:** Features a polished, executive-level layout with a clean corporate color palette and intuitive navigation icons for effortless metric discovery.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

* **Power BI** – Data Modeling & Dashboard Architecture
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Advanced Business Intelligence Analytics
* **Power Query** – ETL, Data Cleaning, and External API Integration
