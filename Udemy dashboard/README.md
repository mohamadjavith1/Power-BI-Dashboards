# 🎓 Udemy Course Market Analysis Dashboard

**Access Live Dashboard Here:** https://tinyurl.com/ysr66yj3

## 📊 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a deep-dive market analysis of Udemy course data to help educators, content creators, and platform analysts decode educational market trends[cite: 9]. The solution transforms raw course listings into actionable strategic insights regarding optimal pricing frameworks, content popularity vectors, and student enrollment demographics[cite: 9].

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Insights

* **Executive Overview:** High-impact KPI cards displaying baseline platform health, including **Total Courses**, **Total Subscribers**, and **Total Revenue Generated**[cite: 9].
* **Subject Area Analysis:** A comparative breakdown of performance across major core categories: *Business Finance, Graphic Design, Musical Instruments, and Web Development*[cite: 9].
* **Engagement & Quality Metrics:** Interactive scatter plots mapping the ratio of *Number of Reviews* against *Number of Subscribers* to gauge overall course quality and student satisfaction levels[cite: 9].
* **Level & Difficulty Insights:** Tailored visualizations uncovering how *Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert* level courses differ significantly in terms of enrollment volume and revenue generation[cite: 9].
* **Content Strategy Analytics:** Deep-dive analysis exploring course duration (*Content Hours*) and its direct correlation with subscriber acquisition and pricing elasticity[cite: 9].

---

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights

* **Data Modeling:** Modeled using an optimized schema designed to easily handle multi-attribute filtering across course levels and subjects without performance lag[cite: 9].
* **Advanced Metrics:** Created robust DAX measures to cleanly isolate total revenue generation and subscriber ratios across highly variable course prices[cite: 9].
* **UI/UX & Scannability:** Designed with clear visual hierarchy, grouping high-level performance cards together before breaking into detailed category slicers[cite: 9].
