This Power BI dashboard provides a deep-dive analysis of Udemy course data to help educators and platform analysts understand market trends. It transforms raw course data into actionable insights regarding pricing strategies, content popularity, and student demographics.

Key Features:

Executive Overview: At-a-glance metrics for Total Courses, Total Subscribers, and Total Revenue Generated.

Subject Analysis: A breakdown of performance across major categories (Business Finance, Graphic Design, Musical Instruments, and Web Development).

Engagement Metrics: Analysis of "Number of Reviews" vs. "Number of Subscribers" to gauge course quality and student satisfaction.

Level & Difficulty Insights: Visualizations showing how Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert level courses differ in enrollment and revenue.

Content Strategy: Analysis of course duration (content hours) and its correlation with subscriber counts
