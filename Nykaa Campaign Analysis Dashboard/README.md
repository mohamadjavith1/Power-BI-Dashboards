\# \*\*💄 Nykaa Fashion Campaign Analysis Dashboard\*\*



\## 📊 Project Overview



This Power BI dashboard analyzes marketing campaign performance for Nykaa Fashion. It provides insights into revenue generation, customer conversions, campaign effectiveness, and channel performance.



The dashboard is designed to help marketing teams optimize campaign strategies, improve ROI, and understand customer engagement across different channels.



\---



\## 📌 Dashboard Structure



\### 🧭 1. Overview



Provides a high-level summary of campaign performance:



\* \*\*Total Revenue:\*\* 29bn

\* \*\*Total Conversions:\*\* 57M

\* \*\*Return on Investment (ROI):\*\* 1.37K

\* \*\*Total Impressions:\*\* 3bn



\*\*Key Insights:\*\*



\* Influencer and Social Media campaigns generate strong revenue

\* Premium and working professionals are key target audiences

\* Revenue shows a declining trend over time



\---



\### 📈 2. Channel Insights



\* \*\*Revenue by Channel\*\*

&#x20; Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, and Google dominate revenue



\* \*\*Conversion by Campaign Type\*\*
