# 💄 Nykaa Fashion Campaign Analysis Dashboard

**Access Live Dashboard Here:** https://tinyurl.com/46y6w88n

## 📊 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes marketing campaign performance for Nykaa Fashion. It delivers strategic insights into revenue generation, customer conversions, campaign effectiveness, and channel performance to help marketing teams optimize future campaign strategies, maximize ROI, and better understand multi-channel customer engagement.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Structure

### 🧭 1. Overview Page
Provides a high-level summary of baseline campaign performance:
* **Total Revenue:** ₹29Bn
* **Total Conversions:** 57M
* **Return on Investment (ROI):** 1.37K%
* **Total Impressions:** 3Bn

**Key Insights:**
* Influencer and Social Media marketing campaigns generate the strongest top-line revenue.
* Premium shoppers and working professionals represent the highest-converting target audiences.
* Historical revenue trends indicate a gradual decline over time, signaling a need for campaign fatigue refresh cycles.

### 📈 2. Channel Insights Page
* **Revenue by Channel:** Identifies that Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, and Google dominate the channel revenue mix.
* **Conversion by Campaign Type:** Confirms that Social Media and Influencer-led campaigns outperform traditional ad frameworks.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzes performance balance across multiple customer demographics.
* **Campaign Cost vs. Revenue:** Deploys advanced scatter/bubble charts to contrast financial efficiency across active campaigns.

### 🎯 3. Campaign Focused Metrics Page
* **Conversion Rate by Campaign Type:** Evaluates direct-response efficiency.
* **Campaign Trend Over Time:** Maps seasonal velocity and performance lifecycles.
* **ROI by Campaign Type:** Ranks marketing strategies by ultimate financial return.
* **Cost per Conversion (CPC):** Identifies efficiency bottlenecks and cost-per-acquisition performance.
