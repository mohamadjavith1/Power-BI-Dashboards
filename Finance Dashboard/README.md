Project Title: Executive Financial Performance Insights Dashboard

Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of organizational financial health, designed to help executives and finance managers track key performance indicators (KPIs) in real-time. By integrating diverse financial metrics, the report transitions from high-level summaries to granular trend analysis, enabling data-driven decision-making.



Key Features \& Visuals

Core Financial Metrics: High-impact cards displaying essential KPIs including Total Sales, Gross Profit, and Net Income.



Economic Context Tracking: Integration of external economic indicators such as inflation rates, interest rates, and GDP growth to provide context for internal performance.



Market Analysis: Dedicated tracking for currency fluctuations (USD/EUR, USD/JPY) and commodity prices like Gold and Crude Oil to monitor market volatility impact.



Trend Analysis: Visualizations highlighting performance over time, specifically focusing on sales growth, spending patterns, and venture capital investment trends.



Operational Health: Monitoring of labor market impacts through unemployment rate tracking alongside corporate profit margins.



Technical Highlights

Data Modeling: Leverages a robust Star Schema to ensure high performance and scalability.



DAX Measures: Includes custom DAX for year-over-year (YoY) growth calculations and dynamic margin analysis.



UI/UX Design: A clean, professional layout utilizing custom icons for intuitive navigation and quick metric identification.
